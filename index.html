Oracle to Snowflake · ELT

Banking data pipeline and reporting platform

Brings the bank’s Oracle customer, account and transaction data into a governed Snowflake warehouse for business and operations teams.

  • Daily batch loads through S3 with COPY INTO, plus file-arrival ingestion with Snowpipe.
  • Incremental, CDC-style processing with Streams and Tasks instead of full reloads.
  • Modular dbt models with tests, orchestrated in Airflow and released through CI/CD.
  • Clustering, warehouse right-sizing and RBAC for speed, cost and data security.

Customer transaction validation and exception management

Validates transactions from biometric banking devices and tracks every failure until it is approved or resolved. I own ingestion, validation and Snowflake publishing.

  • Pandas parsers for XML, CSV and JSON with de-duplication and mandatory-field checks.
  • SQL rules that catch invalid accounts, duplicates and daily or monthly limit breaches.
  • Exception lifecycle with type, severity, status and an approval path for high-value transactions.
  • Idempotent reruns, source-to-target reconciliation and Power BI reporting.
